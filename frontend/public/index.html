Diffusion Models & DDPM Architecture Reference

Model / Concept Core Paradigm Characteristics / Limitations
GAN (Generative Adversarial) Generator vs. Discriminator minimax optimization Prone to mode collapse and training instability.
VAE (Variational Autoencoder) Probabilistic latent space with Reconstruction Loss + KL Divergence Tends to produce blurry outputs due to pixel-space averaging.
Diffusion Model & DDPM Forward Markovian noise addition and Reverse iterative denoising State-of-the-art fidelity, sample diversity, and exact mask inpainting.
1. Encoder

Downsamples input images to extract hierarchical feature representations.

2. Time-Step Embeddings

Informs each U-Net layer of the current diffusion timestep $t$ and noise level.

3. Skip Connections

Directly links encoder stages to decoder stages to retain fine details and layout edges.

4. Decoder

Upsamples feature maps and predicts the residual noise $\epsilon_\theta(x_t, t)$.
